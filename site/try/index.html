How settled is your listening?

Compares your last four weeks against your last year. Runs entirely in this browser — nothing is sent anywhere, because there is nowhere to send it.

Read-only. The single permission requested is user-top-read, which sees your top artists and nothing else — not your playlists, not your email, not your listening history.

Access is by invitation. While this app is in Spotify's development mode they cap it at 25 people, each added by hand. If signing in fails, that is why — you are not on the list yet. Ask and I will add the email on your Spotify account.

Snapshots are saved in this browser only. They do not sync to your other devices, nobody else can see them, and they are lost if you clear site data. Forget everything

No server, no account, no analytics. The only network requests this page makes are to accounts.spotify.com, api.spotify.com, and a CDN for the charting library.

Read the source if you would rather verify that than take my word for it.
